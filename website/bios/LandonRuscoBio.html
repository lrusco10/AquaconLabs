Landon Rusco is an undergraduate student at SUNY Oswego studying Software Engineering. He is the sole Web Developer of the official SOAC website and the "genius engineer" behind SOACWebEdit—a Java desktop application geared towards enabling non-web-designers to seamlessly use generative AI to edit and improve the SOAC website as the organization continues to grow.


He was the President of the Oswego Computer Science Association and is one of the few co-founders of LakerHacks, an annual interstate hackathon held at SUNY Oswego. As a full-stack software engineer, he prides himself on his extensive Unix system expertise and the many educational, professional, and auxiliary programming projects featured on his GitHub. During his free time, he practices Shotokan karate, woodcarving, and steel-forging.
