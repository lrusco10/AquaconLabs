Lyric Mcculloch

Lab Technician
SUNY Oswego

Lyric is the SOAC Lab technician with research interests in conservation genetics, aquatic ecology, and evolutionary biology. They received their B.S. in Biological Sciences from the University of Southern Mississippi, where they conducted research on the population genetics of Gulf sturgeon under the guidance of Brian Kreiser. Their undergraduate thesis focused on sibship analysis of fall- and spring-spawning Gulf sturgeon in the Choctawhatchee River, using microsatellite DNA to analyze population structure. This project inspired Lyric to continue pursuing a career in the field of conservation genetics. They also participated in an NSF REU internship at the University of Cincinnati, where they studied the sensory ecology of Mexican cave tetra populations using immunohistochemistry and behavioral assays.


As the primary SOAC Lab technician, Lyric applies molecular techniques—including gDNA and eDNA extractions, endpoint and quantitative PCR, and NGS library preparations—which are used in parentage-based tagging and invasive species monitoring projects. Lyric is also responsible for training and supervising students in the SOAC Lab.


Outside of the lab, Lyric loves playing board games and music.
